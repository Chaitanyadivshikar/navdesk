Fund accounting · Asset servicing

One number closes the day.

Every fund — mutual, hedge, or private — ends its accounting day by striking a single figure: the net asset value per share. This page rebuilds that number the way a fund administrator's engine does: position by position, accrual by accrual, through fees, capital activity, and swing pricing.

Official NAV / share —

The formula is short. The work isn't.

NAV per share is total assets minus total liabilities, divided by shares outstanding. But none of those three terms is a number anyone types in — each one is built from pricing feeds, day-count accruals, fee terms, and the day's capital activity.

NAV / share = ( Total assets − Total liabilities ) ÷ Shares outstanding

In the worksheet below, only the inputs are editable — every total, accrual, fee, and share count is derived, exactly as it is in production: market values from quantity × price × FX, interest from day-count conventions, fees from the fund's terms, and shares from capital orders dealt at the prior day's price.

The daily cycle behind the strike.

The worksheet above is the last page of a process that runs every valuation day, in order. Miss a step and the number is wrong — and a wrong NAV means every investor transacts at the wrong price.

STEP 1

Capture trades & positions

Pull the day's executed trades and the full position file from the order system; confirm the trade blotter is complete.

STEP 2

Price the book

Apply close-of-day prices and FX rates from vendor feeds; investigate stale, missing, or tolerance-breaching prices before they flow to the ledger.

STEP 3

Accrue income & expenses

Book daily accruals — dividends, coupon interest on day-count conventions, management and performance fees, and operating expenses.

STEP 4

Process capital activity

Deal the day's subscriptions and redemptions at the configured price, updating shares outstanding and cash.

STEP 5

Reconcile

Break-check cash and positions against the custodian and prime broker; clear or explain every break before signing off.

STEP 6

Strike & deliver

Compute NAV per share, apply swing pricing if flows breach the threshold, run tolerance checks, obtain sign-off, and release the official price.
